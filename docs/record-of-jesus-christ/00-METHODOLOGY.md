## Purpose

Compile all verifiable historical, textual, archaeological, and scientific data related to the biblical textual tradition and the historical figure Jesus of Nazareth. Label every claim by evidence tier. Distinguish proof from concept. Do not advocate, debunk as a goal, or conclude divinity.

## Evidence tiers (mandatory)

| Tier | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Verified** | Multiple independent primary sources + physical/scientific corroboration with high confidence |
| **Well-Attested** | Strong primary or early secondary attestation; limited contrary evidence |
| **Circumstantial** | Indirect but coherent supporting evidence |
| **Contested / Scholarly Debate** | Significant specialist disagreement; all major positions with evidence weights |
| **Interpretive Analysis** | Reconstruction beyond raw data; labeled as analysis |
| **Speculative / Unverifiable** | Hypothesis or tradition lacking sufficient support |
| **Literary / Theological Development** | Later interpretive/liturgical/doctrinal elaboration — never as historical fact |

## Tools (historical/scientific instruments only)

- Multiple independent attestation, embarrassment, coherence, contextual credibility
- Nestle-Aland / UBS / INTF manuscript standards; paleographic and ¹⁴C ranges
- Stratigraphy, pottery chronologies, provenance requirements
- Observational cosmology (CMB, expansion, BBN) as science; no teleology as fact
- Full debate status for Josephus TF, James passage, Tacitus, etc.

## Out of scope

- Advocacy of belief or disbelief
- Prophecy fulfillment as historical method
- Mapping ancient material to modern politics without forced primary link
- Theological conclusions (divinity, resurrection as fact) presented as science/history
- Any personal operator identity

## Source hierarchy

1. Primary texts and inscriptions  
2. Peer-reviewed scientific/archaeological literature  
3. Critical editions and standard reference works  
4. Survey monographs used for orientation, not sole proof  

## Update triggers

- New major manuscript publication  
- New peer-reviewed dig or radiocarbon result  
- Material shift in scholarly consensus  
- Residual bias or tier error discovery
